Privacy Policy

Effective & last updated: November 19, 2026 · Applies to myIPON Web (my-ipon.vercel.app) and the myIPON Android app.

myIPON is a private, non-custodial group savings record tracker. This Privacy Policy explains what personal and savings-group data we process, how Google and Facebook Sign-In work, how long data is retained, and how you can delete your data at any time.

1. Information we process

myIPON may store your formal name, email address, mobile number, optional profile picture, optional payout receiving account details (channel, account name, and account number), policy consent version and timestamp, assigned roles, savings-group memberships, contributions, expenses, withdrawals, annual archived summaries, payment channel tags, reconciliation records, in-record comments, direct messages, media attachments, announcement reactions, feature requests, bug reports, and security-related account events.

2. Google & Facebook Sign-In (OAuth)

When you choose Continue with Google, Create with Google, Continue with Facebook, or Create with Facebook, authentication is processed securely through our identity provider (Clerk) using OAuth 2.0:

  • Data requested: Only basic profile identity fields needed to create or match your account — your verified email address, full name, and optional profile photo.
  • How it is used: Strictly to verify your identity, sign you in, and pre-fill your myIPON profile.
  • What we never do: myIPON never posts to your social media accounts, never reads your private messages, contacts, or friend lists, and never sells or shares your personal data with third-party advertisers.

3. How information is used & media storage

  • Authenticate accounts (including email OTP verification, OAuth social sign-in, password recovery, and optional TOTP two-factor authentication) and enforce database row-level isolation per group and role.
  • Calculate monthly summaries, calendar progress, wallet balances, CSV/print reports, annual roll-up summaries, and audit logs.
  • Store uploaded images, voice notes, documents, and receipts inside dedicated cloud object storage buckets (ipon-media, chat-attachments, announcements, record-comments) referenced by URL rather than storing raw binary blobs or Base64 strings inside database tables.
  • Deliver real-time device and in-app notifications (arriving on your phone as long as myIPON is kept in Recent Apps and not swiped away) for payment reviews, chat messages, discussion replies, account status changes, and announcements. Activity alerts are not sent to your email; email is reserved strictly for OTP verification codes, password recovery links, dormant savings reminders (90 days idle), and 7-day inactivity/retention warnings.
  • Summarize high-level platform updates in Super Admin release notes without exposing private group financial balances, and display optional requester credit on implemented feature requests when attribution is enabled.

4. Browser storage & device alerts

The app stores session credentials, cross-tab sync keys, your light or dark theme selection, per-account policy consent cache keys, periodic data-retention reminder timestamps, and optional Web Push subscription tokens locally on your browser or Android app so settings stay consistent. Keep myIPON in your phone's Recent Apps (do not remove it from Recent Apps) to receive incoming messages and activity notifications in real time (activity alerts are not emailed). Device alerts can be disabled anytime in your device settings or Account preferences.

5. Who can access your data

Access is strictly limited by assigned role and active savings group. Authorized group administrators and treasurers review permitted group workspace activity and payout info. Platform administrators manage system health, storage hygiene, account access, bug reports, and feature requests, and are not intended to view private group savings ledgers.

6. Detailed data retention & automatic deletion

To keep the app fast and storage clean while protecting active group savers permanently, automated system cleanup runs nightly at 12:00 AM Manila time:

Ongoing
Active savings & ongoing goals (Immune)Active goals (savings_goals) and unbinned savings records (contributions, withdrawals, expenses, contribution_periods) are never automatically erased while contributions or goal updates remain active.
90d → 6m
Dormant / forgotten savings (Email + 7d notice)Groups with zero deposits or goal updates receive an email reminder at 90 days and a final warning 7 days prior to 6 months before dormant ledgers and untouched goals move into the 30-day Bin.
7 days
Login rate-limit & throttle logsFailed sign-in attempt counters (auth_login_throttles) expire automatically after 7 days.
30-day Bin
Done / reset batches, binned goals, members & noticesCompleted goals or reset savings batches (savings_cycle_batches), deleted announcements, and soft-deleted member profiles remain recoverable for 30 days with an exact remaining-days countdown before permanent purge from PostgreSQL and cloud Storage.
30 days
Call logs & read notificationsVoice/video call session metadata (call_sessions) and read alerts (notifications) are purged automatically after 30 days.
90 days
Direct & group messages & chat Storage filesMessages (messages) and uploaded media in chat-attachments older than 90 days are deleted automatically. Messages, notes, and replies are capped at 500 characters.
90 days
Unread alerts, rejected receipts & logsUnread notifications, rejected or voided receipt attachments (alongside their Storage files), audit_logs, and account_activity clear automatically after 90 days.
6 months
Inactive accounts (7-day warning email)Accounts inactive for 6 months (zero logins, member contributions, or active group savings activity) receive an email warning 7 days prior to permanent removal (shared groups promote an active successor automatically).
1+ year
Annual financial roll-upsConfirmed transactions older than 1 year can be exported to CSV first and summarized into permanent financial_annual_archives totals—never erased silently.

Confirming permanent group deletion immediately clears that group's workspace data. Users are also shown a periodic in-app storage reminder every 14 days and can review retention rules anytime under Account Preferences.

7. User data deletion

Need to delete your myIPON account or Facebook/Google Sign-In data? Follow our step-by-step User Data Deletion Instructions to delete your workspace data inside myIPON, disconnect Facebook or Google access, or request full permanent account removal by email.
