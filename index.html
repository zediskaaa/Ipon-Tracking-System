Security

Built to protect your group's records

Security is enforced by the database itself, not just by the screens you click.

Group isolation

Row-level security keeps every group's data separate. No group can ever see another's records.

RLS: current_group_id()

Two-factor sign-in

Turn on authenticator-app codes for an extra layer of protection on your account.

TOTP 2FA: 6-Digit Code

Locked records

Confirmed entries cannot be silently edited. Corrections are made through voids with a reason.

Audit: Trigger-Enforced Void

Verified accounts

New signups require email verification with a one-time code, so no one can register using an address they do not own.

10-Min Email OTP

IPON never holds your money. It is a record-keeping tool only — funds stay with your group's chosen treasurer, bank, or e-wallet.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does IPON store or transfer money?

No. IPON is a non-custodial ledger. It records what your group saves and spends, but the actual money stays wherever your group keeps it — cash, bank, or e-wallet.

How much does it cost?

IPON is free to use while it is in active development. Voluntary donations to the developer are welcome but never required, and they are never mixed with group savings.

Do my members need to install an app?

No. IPON runs in any modern browser. On phones, members can add it to their home screen for an app-like experience.

How do I start a savings group?

Click "Sign up", verify your email with a 6-digit code, and set your password. Your account is ready immediately to create and manage your group.

I was invited by my group admin. What now?

Check your email for your sign-in details, then click "Sign in". You'll be asked to set your own password on first login.
