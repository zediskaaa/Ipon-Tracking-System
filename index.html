FAQ

Questions, answered

Does myIPON store or transfer money?

No. myIPON is a non-custodial ledger. It records contributions, payment channels, payout accounts, and wallet balances, while real money stays in your group's cash box, bank, or e-wallet.

How much does it cost?

myIPON is free to use while it is in active development. Voluntary donations to the developer are welcome but never required, and they are never mixed with group savings.

Do my members need to install an app?

No. myIPON runs in any modern browser on phone or desktop. Android users can also install our dedicated .apk app or add myIPON to their home screen for background push notifications.

How do I start a savings group?

Click "Sign up", verify your email with a 6-digit code, and set your password. You can create and manage your group immediately with no waiting period.

I was invited by my group admin. What now?

Check your email for your temporary sign-in details, then click "Sign in". You will set your own password and finish your profile details on first login.

Can admins transfer ownership or recover removed members?

Yes. Group Owners can transfer group ownership to another administrator in Settings, restore soft-deleted members from the 30-day recovery bin, or permanently delete unused groups with confirmation.
