Effective & last updated: November 22, 2026 · Applies to myIPON Web (my-ipon.vercel.app) and the myIPON Android app.

1. Purpose & non-custodial scope

myIPON records group and solo savings activity—along with optional personal 50/30/20 budget allocations and Monthly Income & Expense Monitoring—entered by authorized users. It is a tracking system only and does not hold, transfer, receive, or safeguard money.

Recording payment methods (such as GCash, Maya, Bank, or Cash), reference numbers, optional payout receiving accounts, personal budget or monthly income/expense entries, or wallet reconciliation balances helps you and your group track where funds sit offline; myIPON never initiates or processes real financial transfers.

2. Accounts, Google Sign-In & your responsibilities

  • Verify your email with a 6-digit one-time code or sign in with an authorized Google account via Clerk OAuth 2.0, choose a unique @username, use accurate formal profile information, and keep your sign-in credentials, optional two-factor authenticator (TOTP) codes, and optional new-device email verification codes private.
  • Use only accounts, solo workspaces, and shared savings-group records you are authorized to access, and respect group invite links/codes, role permissions, and member change-request rules.
  • Verify important balances, member payouts, budget deductions, and excess carry-over entries against physical or e-wallet receipts.
  • Accept the current Terms of Service and Privacy Policy upon your first sign-in or after major policy updates.

3. Messaging, chat privacy, badges & profile customization

  • myIPON supports direct messages (defaulting to friends-only with optional message requests and blocked accounts), group chats with Owner/Admin/Member roles, voice messages, and media attachments. The platform creator may remain reachable for verification and support inquiries.
  • Optional chat privacy controls include Vanishing Messages (automatically and permanently deleted once the chosen read timer expires), Anonymous Chat Mode in direct and group chats (masking participant names, profile photos, and previews while active, and permanently deleting all messages and linked alerts from that anonymous session when turned off), forwarding restrictions, screenshot notices, and native screen-capture blocking on the myIPON Android app.
  • Verified badges & profile effects: Standard Blue verified badges and profile visual effects may be granted permanently, for a set duration, or on a scheduled date. Permanent standard verified badges are automatically removed after 2 months of account inactivity; time-limited badges and effect trials expire automatically at their scheduled end time. Lifetime Creator badges are exempt from inactivity removal, and exclusive profile effects are granted only by platform administrators.

4. Notifications & email delivery scope

Notifications and direct chat messages arrive in real time on your device as long as myIPON stays in your phone's Recent Apps (do not swipe it away or remove it from Recent Apps). On the myIPON Android app and supported browsers, push alerts are delivered using Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens and optional Web Push subscriptions; keeping myIPON in Recent Apps and allowing background usage ensures reliable real-time alerts for new messages, payment reviews, contribution updates, replies, friend requests, and announcements. Routine chat messages, reactions, and announcement alerts are not sent via email.

Automated emails are reserved strictly for account security, invitations, scheduled unpaid savings reminders, and retention notices: 6-digit email verification (OTP) codes, optional new-device login OTP codes, password recovery links, account status notices, workspace invitations, milestone unpaid contribution & savings goal reminders (when notifications are enabled: 3 days before due date, on the due date, 1 day overdue, then weekly while overdue, and at most once every 7 days per unpaid goal), dormant savings check-in reminders (90 days idle), and 7-day prior warnings before 6-month inactive account or abandoned savings retention.

5. Approvals, ongoing savings protection & dormant retention

Only confirmed entries affect tracked savings totals. Rejected and voided entries carry mandatory reasons and remain recorded in the group audit trail (retained for 90 days) for accountability.

Ongoing savings & active goals are never deleted while you keep saving, no matter how many months or years it takes your group or solo workspace to reach a target. Active savings records, confirmed ledgers, personal budget plans, Monthly Income & Expense Monitoring records, and ongoing goals stay intact as long as contributions or goal activity continue.

Forgotten or abandoned savings policy: To prevent completely abandoned workspaces from filling storage indefinitely while giving fair notice, workspaces with zero contributions, transactions, or goal updates receive a friendly email reminder at 90 days, and if still untouched by 6 months receive a 7-day prior email notice before dormant records and untouched goals move into the 30-day Recycle Bin (still recoverable for 30 days before permanent database & cloud storage removal).

Group Owners and Administrators manage invitations, role assignments, ownership transfers, member permissions, the 30-day soft-delete member recovery bin, completed goals, finished batch resets, and permanent workspace deletions. When a goal or finished savings batch is marked Done / Removed, it moves into the 30-day Recycle Bin with a visible remaining-days countdown before automatic removal from the database and cloud storage. Completed financial years older than 1 year can also be exported to CSV first and rolled up into permanent annual net-savings summaries (financial_annual_archives).

6. Uploads, character limits & automatic cleanup

Profile photos, profile covers, group QR codes, receipts, announcement media, record discussion replies, voice notes, and chat attachments must stay lawful and relevant to workspace tracking. Never post PINs, OTP codes, passwords, or private banking credentials. Chat messages, transaction notes, and record replies are capped at 500 characters, short profile bios are capped at 58 characters, and Group Announcements remain unlimited so full notices stay complete.

To keep database and cloud storage responsive without disturbing active savers, automated nightly maintenance runs every midnight (12:00 AM Manila time) strictly on expired temporary/binned data:
