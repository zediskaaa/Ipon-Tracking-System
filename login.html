Terms of use

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Purpose

IPON records savings-related activity entered by authorized users. It is a tracking system only and does not hold, transfer, receive, or safeguard money.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information and keep your sign-in credentials private.
  • Use only accounts and savings-group records you are authorized to access.
  • Verify important totals against receipts or the group's official records.

Approvals and records

Only confirmed entries affect tracked savings. Rejected and voided entries may remain in the audit history for accountability.

Development availability

The system is under active development. Features may be incomplete, changed, unavailable, or contain errors. Do not treat IPON as a bank, wallet, or the sole official financial record.

Information we process

IPON may store your name, email address, mobile number, role, savings-group membership, submitted records, review activity, and security-related account events.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, enforce role and group access, display savings records, process approvals, send in-app notifications, and maintain an audit trail.

Who can access it

Access is limited by assigned role and savings group. Authorized administrators may review relevant account and activity information. Platform administrators manage account access but are not intended to view a group's savings records.

Security and retention

Reasonable technical controls are used, but no developing system can guarantee uninterrupted or error-free security. Records may be retained for audit and accountability purposes.

Questions

For privacy or account concerns, contact your assigned administrator.
