Terms of use

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Purpose & non-custodial scope

myIPON records savings-related activity entered by authorized users. It is a tracking system only and does not hold, transfer, receive, or safeguard money.

Recording payment methods (such as GCash, Maya, Bank, or Cash), reference numbers, optional payout receiving accounts, or wallet reconciliation balances helps your group track where funds sit offline; myIPON never initiates or processes real financial transfers.

Accounts & your responsibilities

  • Verify your email with a one-time code, use accurate formal profile information, and keep your sign-in credentials and optional two-factor authenticator codes private.
  • Use only accounts, workspaces, and savings-group records you are authorized to access.
  • Verify important balances, member payouts, and excess carry-over entries against physical or e-wallet receipts.

Approvals, audit trail & group lifecycle

Only confirmed entries affect tracked savings totals. Rejected and voided entries carry mandatory reasons and remain recorded in the group audit trail for accountability.

Group Owners and Administrators manage invitations, role assignments, ownership transfers, the 30-day soft-delete member recovery bin, and permanent group deletions.

Uploads, discussions & acceptable use

Profile photos, announcement media, record discussion replies, and uploaded attachments (images, videos, documents, or bug screenshots) must stay lawful and relevant to group tracking. Never post PINs, OTP codes, passwords, or private banking credentials.

Development availability

The system is under active development. Features, release notes, and automated alerts may be updated, incomplete, or temporarily unavailable. Do not treat myIPON as a bank, wallet, or the sole official financial record.

Information we process

myIPON may store your formal name, email address, mobile number, optional profile picture, optional payout receiving account details (channel, account name, and account number), assigned roles, savings-group memberships, contributions, expenses, withdrawals, payment channel tags, reconciliation records, in-record comments and attachments, announcement reactions, feature requests, bug reports, and security-related account events.

How information is used

  • Authenticate accounts (including email OTP verification, password recovery, and optional TOTP two-factor authentication) and enforce database row-level isolation per group and role.
  • Calculate monthly summaries, calendar progress, wallet balances, CSV/print reports, and audit logs.
  • Deliver email, in-app, and optional Web Push notifications for payment reviews, discussion replies, account status changes, and announcements.
  • Summarize high-level platform updates in Super Admin release notes without exposing private group financial balances, and display optional requester credit on implemented feature requests when attribution is enabled.

Browser storage & device alerts

The app stores session credentials, cross-tab sync keys, your light or dark theme selection, and optional Web Push subscription tokens locally on your browser or Android app so settings stay consistent. Push alerts can be disabled anytime in your device settings or Account preferences.

Who can access it

Access is strictly limited by assigned role and active savings group. Authorized group administrators and treasurers review permitted group workspace activity and payout info. Platform administrators manage system health, account access, bug reports, and feature requests, and are not intended to view private group savings ledgers.

Security, retention & deletion

Reasonable technical controls are used, though no developing system can guarantee uninterrupted or error-free security. Soft-deleted member records may remain recoverable for up to 30 days, and audit records are retained while a group remains active. Confirming permanent group deletion clears that group's workspace data.
