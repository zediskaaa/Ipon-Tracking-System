Terms of use

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Purpose & non-custodial scope

myIPON records savings-related activity entered by authorized users. It is a tracking system only and does not hold, transfer, receive, or safeguard money.

Recording payment methods (such as GCash, Maya, Bank, or Cash), reference numbers, optional payout receiving accounts, or wallet reconciliation balances helps your group track where funds sit offline; myIPON never initiates or processes real financial transfers.

Accounts & your responsibilities

  • Verify your email with a one-time code, use accurate formal profile information, and keep your sign-in credentials and optional two-factor authenticator (TOTP) codes private.
  • Use only accounts, workspaces, and savings-group records you are authorized to access.
  • Verify important balances, member payouts, and excess carry-over entries against physical or e-wallet receipts.
  • Accept the current Terms of Use and Privacy Policy upon your first sign-in or after major policy updates.

Notifications & email delivery scope

Notifications and direct chat messages are delivered in-app only. You must open or keep myIPON running on your device or browser to view and receive real-time alerts for new messages, payment reviews, contribution updates, replies, and announcements. Activity notifications are not sent via email and are not guaranteed to arrive when the app is completely closed on a phone.

Automated emails are reserved strictly for account security and retention notices: 6-digit email verification (OTP) codes, password recovery links, dormant savings check-in reminders (90 days idle), and 7-day prior warnings before 6-month inactive account or abandoned savings retention.

Approvals, ongoing savings protection & dormant retention

Only confirmed entries affect tracked savings totals. Rejected and voided entries carry mandatory reasons and remain recorded in the group audit trail (retained for 90 days) for accountability.

Ongoing savings & active goals are never deleted while you keep saving, no matter how many months or years it takes your group to reach a target. Active savings records, confirmed ledgers, and ongoing goals stay intact as long as contributions or goal activity continue.

Forgotten or abandoned savings policy: To prevent completely abandoned workspaces from filling storage indefinitely while giving fair notice, groups with zero contributions, transactions, or goal updates receive a friendly email reminder at 90 days, and if still untouched by 6 months receive a 7-day prior email notice before dormant records and untouched goals move into the 30-day Recycle Bin (still recoverable for 30 days before permanent database & cloud storage removal).

Group Owners and Administrators manage invitations, role assignments, ownership transfers, the 30-day soft-delete member recovery bin, completed goals, finished batch resets, and permanent group deletions. When a goal or finished savings batch is marked Done / Removed, it moves into the 30-day Recycle Bin with a visible remaining-days countdown before automatic removal from the database and cloud storage. Completed financial years older than 1 year can also be exported to CSV first and rolled up into permanent annual net-savings summaries (financial_annual_archives).

Uploads, character limits & automatic cleanup

Profile photos, group QR codes, receipts, announcement media, record discussion replies, and chat attachments must stay lawful and relevant to group tracking. Never post PINs, OTP codes, passwords, or private banking credentials. Chat messages, transaction notes, and record replies are capped at 500 characters (Group Announcements remain unlimited so full notices stay complete).

To keep database and cloud storage responsive without disturbing active savers, automated nightly maintenance runs every midnight (12:00 AM Manila time) strictly on expired temporary/binned data:

Development availability

The system is under active development. Features, release notes, and automated alerts may be updated, incomplete, or temporarily unavailable. Do not treat myIPON as a bank, wallet, or the sole official financial record.

Information we process

myIPON may store your formal name, email address, mobile number, optional profile picture, optional payout receiving account details (channel, account name, and account number), policy consent version and timestamp, assigned roles, savings-group memberships, contributions, expenses, withdrawals, annual archived summaries, payment channel tags, reconciliation records, in-record comments, direct messages, media attachments, announcement reactions, feature requests, bug reports, and security-related account events.

How information is used & media storage

  • Authenticate accounts (including email OTP verification, password recovery, and optional TOTP two-factor authentication) and enforce database row-level isolation per group and role.
  • Calculate monthly summaries, calendar progress, wallet balances, CSV/print reports, annual roll-up summaries, and audit logs.
  • Store uploaded images, voice notes, documents, and receipts inside dedicated cloud object storage buckets (ipon-media, chat-attachments, announcements, record-comments) referenced by URL rather than storing raw binary blobs or Base64 strings inside database tables.
  • Deliver in-app notifications (and device banners while myIPON stays open) for payment reviews, chat messages, discussion replies, account status changes, and announcements. Activity alerts are not sent to your email; email is reserved strictly for OTP verification codes, password recovery links, dormant savings reminders (90 days idle), and 7-day inactivity/retention warnings.
  • Summarize high-level platform updates in Super Admin release notes without exposing private group financial balances, and display optional requester credit on implemented feature requests when attribution is enabled.

Browser storage & device alerts

The app stores session credentials, cross-tab sync keys, your light or dark theme selection, per-account policy consent cache keys, periodic data-retention reminder timestamps, and optional Web Push subscription tokens locally on your browser or Android app so settings stay consistent. Open myIPON directly to check incoming messages and activity notifications (alerts are in-app only and not emailed). Device alerts can be disabled anytime in your device settings or Account preferences.

Who can access it

Access is strictly limited by assigned role and active savings group. Authorized group administrators and treasurers review permitted group workspace activity and payout info. Platform administrators manage system health, storage hygiene, account access, bug reports, and feature requests, and are not intended to view private group savings ledgers.
