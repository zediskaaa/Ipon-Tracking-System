User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Applies to myIPON accounts, Google Sign-In, and Facebook Login.

You can delete your myIPON data and disconnect your Facebook or Google Sign-In link at any time using the methods below.

1. Request full account & OAuth data deletion

To permanently delete your entire myIPON account, profile, connected Google/Facebook sign-in identity, uploaded profile photo, and personal records:

  1. Send an email from your registered account email address to zedrickdemonteverde1@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line myIPON Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your full name and the email address used to sign in to myIPON (including Google or Facebook Sign-In).
Direct deletion request email We verify account ownership and permanently remove your profile, OAuth connection, notifications, and uploaded personal files within 7 business days, then send a confirmation reply.
Email Data Deletion Request

2. Delete workspace or profile data inside myIPON

  • Profile details & media: Sign in at myIPON, open Account, and clear optional payout receiving details, profile photo, or social links immediately.
  • Savings group workspaces: Group Owners and Administrators can open Settings and confirm permanent group deletion to immediately erase that group's savings workspace data.

3. Revoke Facebook Login or Google Sign-In access

You can also revoke myIPON's sign-in access directly from your Facebook or Google account settings:

  • Facebook: Go to Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → select myIPON → click Remove.
  • Google: Visit Google Account Third-Party Connections → select myIPON → click Delete all connections you have with myIPON.

4. What data is deleted & timeline

  • Deleted upon account removal: Your user profile (name, email, phone number, payout details), authentication & OAuth links, profile avatar/cover media, notification tokens, direct message records, and personal security logs.
  • Shared group accounting integrity: If you belong to an active shared savings group, group-owned ledger totals remain intact for the other active members while your personal account identity is removed or reassigned according to group ownership rules.
  • Processing timeline: Active records are removed within 7 business days of verification; any items previously placed in the 30-day Recycle Bin purge automatically within 30 days.

For complete retention schedules, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
